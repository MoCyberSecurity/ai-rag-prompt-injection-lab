Company HR Policies:

1. Holiday Entitlement:
Employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per year, excluding public holidays.

2. Sick Leave:
Employees must notify their manager before 9am on the first day of absence.

3. Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.

4. Confidentiality:
Employee data, including salary and personal details, must not be disclosed without proper authorisation.
